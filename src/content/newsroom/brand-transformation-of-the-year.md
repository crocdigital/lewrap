# Brand Transformation of the Year 2026!

A fresh new look, exciting new flavours and an award-winning transformation!

We're proud to announce that LeWrap has been awarded Brand Transformation of the Year at the 2026 QSR Media Awards!

Over the past 12 months, we've taken LeWrap to a whole new level with a fresh new store design, exciting menu additions, updated branding and more stores opening across Australia.

While we've changed our look, one thing remains the same - our passion for serving all things good and fresh!

This exciting win follows our recent recognition as 2026 Franchisor of the Year by the Franchise Council of Australia, making it two major industry awards in one incredible year!

A huge thank you to our amazing teams and loyal customers for being part of our journey.

The best is yet to come!
