## LeWrap Wins National Franchise Award

We're excited to share some incredible news — **LeWrap has been named Franchisor of the Year (Under 25 Units) at the 2026 Franchise Industry Awards, presented by the Franchise Council of Australia.**

This national award recognises outstanding businesses that demonstrate excellence in leadership, innovation, growth, and customer experience.

For us, this recognition reflects something we've always believed: great food, great people, and a commitment to doing things the right way make all the difference.

#### A Recognition of Our People

This award belongs to every member of the LeWrap family.

From our store teams who serve customers every day, to our franchise partners who bring passion and dedication to their local communities, this achievement is the result of countless people working together to create the LeWrap experience our customers know and love.

#### Thank You to Our Customers

Most importantly, we'd like to thank our customers.

Whether you've been visiting LeWrap for years or you've only recently discovered us, your support has helped us grow into the brand we are today. Every wrap, salad and plate purchased has contributed to this journey.

#### Looking Ahead

While we're incredibly proud of this achievement, we're even more excited about what's next.

We'll continue focusing on what matters most:

* Veggies cut fresh daily
* Meat cooked fresh to order
* Friendly service
* Supporting our local communities

Winning Franchisor of the Year is a proud milestone for LeWrap, and we're grateful to everyone who has been part of our journey so far.

Thank you for your support, we can't wait to share what's next.
